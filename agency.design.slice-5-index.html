Every screen has a Prototype menu (bottom) to switch its states, and ?state=<name> opens one directly. The demo data is shared with the Abstract and client portals on this origin: a proposal, a withdrawal or a justification made here shows there. Demo data reset.

The demo walk (P1)

  1. Vacancies: three released to TechForce Recruitment, each with its open positions, your pay rate and your release date. VAC-1041 is at your limit (2 of 2).
  2. Proposals: open PRP-1006 (Daniel Osei, Submitted) and Withdraw it. VAC-1041 drops to 1 of 2.
  3. VAC-1041: Propose Candidate, choose Amira Hassan, write the notes, Submit Proposal. It reads Submitted; edit its notes.
  4. Candidates: Add Candidate at a branch; it lists under Candidates and can be proposed.
  5. Bookings: BK-2210 — the 28 Sep week is invoiced and shows pay; the 5 Oct week shows none. Justify Wed 7 Oct; it reads Justified with your reason (and on the client's and Abstract's week).
  6. Payroll: Kofi Mensah's statement for the 28 Sep week, with pay; switch View as role to Finance or Recruiter to see the menu change.

IDs refer to Part C of the slice 5 design brief. P1 = the demo path.
