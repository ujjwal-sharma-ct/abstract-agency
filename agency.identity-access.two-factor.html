Two-step verification

Off

After your password, you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Your account stays protected even if someone learns your password.

  • You need an authenticator app

    Any app that shows 6-digit codes works, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.

  • You're asked at every sign-in

    There is no way to skip the code on a device you use often.

  • It stays on

    You can't turn it off yourself. If you lose your phone and your backup codes, an administrator at your organisation can reset it.

    Backup codes

    A backup code signs you in when you can't use your authenticator app. Each works once. They can't be shown again, so if you've lost them or used most of them, create a new set.

    New phone, or lost your phone and your backup codes?

    An administrator at your organisation can reset two-step verification, and can ask Abstract if they can't. A reset signs you out everywhere; you then set it up again if your organisation requires it, or whenever you choose. You can't turn it off or reset it yourself.
